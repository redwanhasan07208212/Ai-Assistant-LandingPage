AI in plain words

AI assistant
Software that understands requests in everyday language and helps you do things.
Chatbot
An assistant you talk to by typing, such as on Messenger or a website.
Prompt
The instruction you give an AI. Clearer instructions give better answers.
Automation
A task that runs by itself, like saving every new order to a sheet.
Hallucination
When an AI confidently says something wrong. Always check important facts.

5 prompts to try today

Paste these into any AI chat and fill in the brackets.

“Write a polite reply to this customer complaint, apologizing and offering a solution: [paste message].”
“Write a short, friendly product description for [product] that mentions [3 features].”
“List 10 questions customers might ask about [my business], with short answers.”
“Translate this message into natural Bangla for customers: [text].”
“Summarize these orders into a short daily report: [paste orders].”

Tip: add who you are, who the customer is, and the tone you want.

Starter FAQ template

Write answers to these 8 questions and your assistant (or your team) can reply to most customers instantly.
